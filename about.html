About SyChro

SyChro is a multilevel timeline that allows you to explore all the factors that have historically contributed to the emergence of a holistic, multidisciplinary, and systemic approach to thinking, touching on environmental, social, economic, educational, and philosophical issues. The more you learn, the more you'll see the world from a new perspective!

SyChro began as research by Serena Treppiedi and Alessandra Savina into the history of systemic disciplines, a body of knowledge that is vast, scattered across fields and institutions, and hard to grasp as a whole. The platform gathers a curated database of milestones in Systems Thinking, System Dynamics, Systemic Design, and Sustainability, and arranges them on five parallel timelines (general history, institutions, documentation, events, and definitions) so that developments across different dimensions can be read side by side. It is designed for researchers, educators, professionals, and students. Its aim is to offer the systemic community a shared, open reference point that encourages exchange across disciplines and supports a shift from linear thinking toward a systemic, collaborative, and regenerative worldview. Future developments include detailed entry pages with multimedia and external links, thematic filters, visible connections between milestones, a mobile version, and user accounts for personal collections and verified contributions.
